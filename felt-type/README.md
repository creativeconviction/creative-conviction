# Felt Type

Not all of us can make words mean things.
Type something perfectly ordinary and the letters will overreact on your behalf.

Write a sentence and the words that carry a feeling move as you type them: *fall* tumbles, *tremble* shakes, *world* turns like a globe. Press **Done**, click any word to try another way of showing the same feeling, and export the result as a PNG or GIF. Below the writing box are passages from public-domain books, set the same way.

## Privacy

- Nothing you write leaves your browser, and nothing is stored in it. Close the tab and it is gone.
- The page makes no network requests after it loads: no analytics, no cookies, no third-party fonts or scripts.
- Exports are made on your device.

## Files

- `index.html`: the whole site, one page.
- `fonts/`: Archivo and Courier Prime, both under the SIL Open Font License (`fonts/OFL-*.txt`). Apple devices use American Typewriter for labels where it is installed.

The page is built from `felt-type/page.html` in the creative-conviction repository with `python3 build.py`.
