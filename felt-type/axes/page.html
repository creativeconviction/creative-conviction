Felt Axes

An experiment for Felt Type. Each word sits somewhere on seven feeling axes, worked out from what the word means. Its letters are set from where it lands: heavier, softer, faster, bigger, hotter. Nothing here is a hand-made list.

Behind the word

Specimens

How it works: 38,246 everyday words were placed on each axis by comparing them with two groups of opposite words, such as calm, relaxed, peaceful against frantic, wild, furious. The word positions come from ConceptNet Numberbatch, word vectors built from common-sense knowledge. Weight, softness, slant and size come from the font's own variable axes. The strongest axis picks the motion.

Left out on purpose: names, nationalities, religions and other identity words. Word vectors learn stereotypes about them, so they stay plain. Each word has one position whatever the sentence, so "cold coffee" and "cold person" look the same.
