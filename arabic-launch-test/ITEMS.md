# Study items

Everything the study is made of: what the models generate, what people need to supply, and what gets voted on.

## Fixed inputs (same for every model)

| Item | File | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Brand | **Qareeb (قريب, "nearby")**: understood the same way in every Arabic variety, not tied to a country | done |
| Brief (English + Arabic) | `prompts/en/brief.md`, `prompts/ar/brief.md` | done; **native check of the Arabic brief** |
| Task prompts: landing page, campaign, support persona | `prompts/{en,ar}/*.md` | done |
| Cities | Riyadh (Saudi/Gulf), Cairo (Egyptian), Casablanca (Moroccan Darija) | done |
| Scripted customer messages, 6 per city, written in local dialect | `markets.json` → `user_turns` | drafted; **native check needed** |
| Billboard headlines, 4 per city (incl. one mixing Arabic, Latin letters, % and digits) | `markets.json` → `headlines` | drafted; **native check needed** |
| Dialect marker word lists | `markets.json` → `markers` | drafted; **native check needed** |

## Generated by models

5 text models (Claude Opus 5.5, GPT-5.6 Sol, Gemini 3.1 Pro, DeepSeek V4 Pro, Mistral Saba) × 3 cities × 2 brief languages = **30 cells**.

| Item | Per cell | Total | Folder |
|---|---|---|---|
| Landing page (HTML) | 1 | 30 | `outputs/landing/` |
| Campaign: big idea, key visual, palette, type, tone + headline, subhead, CTA, 3 ad lines, app-store blurb, 2 push notifications, 3 onboarding screens | 1 | 30 | `outputs/concept/` |
| Support chat (6 customer turns → 6 replies) | 1 | 30 chats / 180 replies | `outputs/persona/` |
| Billboard render test: 12 fixed headlines × 7 image models (GPT Image 2.6, Nano Banana 2.1, Ideogram 4.5, Recraft 4.1 Pro, Seedream 5 Pro, Qwen Image 3, FLUX 2 Max) | – | 84 | `outputs/images/render/` |
| Campaign hero images: each campaign's own image prompt + headline × 3 image models | – | 90 | `outputs/images/concept/` |

Every item's model ID and timestamp is logged in `outputs/runs.jsonl`.

## Supplied by people (needed before voting)

| Item | Who | Time | File |
|---|---|---|---|
| Native check of drafts above | 1 native speaker per city | ~20 min each | `markets.json`, `prompts/ar/brief.md` |
| **Human baseline**: the same campaign copy + the 6 support replies, written by hand | 1 native writer per city, ideally someone who writes marketing | ~45 min each | `human-baseline/{city}.json` |
| Voters | 5–10 native speakers per city | ~10 min each | voting page (next step) |

The human baseline is what makes the voting meaningful: voters compare AI work with real local writing, not just AI with AI.

## How each layer is scored

| Layer | Scored by | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Landing pages | **Code**: 14 browser measurements + screenshots | Objective; no judgment needed |
| Billboard text | **Code** (similarity to the requested text) on a read-back by Claude, which makes no image model; **people** check a sample | Vision models can misread Arabic, so humans verify |
| Dialect | **Code** (word markers) + **two judges from other labs** (no model grades its own lab) | Kept as a cross-check against people |
| Concept EN vs AR | **Judges from other labs** + trope keyword counts | Cross-check |
| Voice, persona, persuasion, image legibility | **People: blind A/B voting** | The final word; AI-vs-human agreement is reported as a finding |
